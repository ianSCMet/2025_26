# 1. Contracts  

## Steam Distribution Agreement (SDA) & Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA)

For an SDA the steam supply agreement, O&M contracts, SLA and access easement, if all these five aspects are in place, then steam distribution agreements, Differently NDA's will require a steam representative to sign the document. The Steam Distribution agreement being a legally binding contract between steam and the user stating that steam will take 30% of the total revenue earned throughout the game's lifetime and a £100 fee is taken before the game is released also in the contract it states that there are revenue tiers. These tiers are Steam takes a 30% cut of revenue, decreasing to 25% for earnings over $10 million, and 20% for sales over $50 million. This is relevant because any paid content including the game or future DLC will fall under a contract agreement which will mean a percentage of earnings will be taken. Price brackets will also be taken into consideration due to the more profit the game earns, the less money steam would take. The NDA section of the game will mean that all development processes and ideas are to be strictly confidential while working towards releasing the game, this is to avoid potential lawsuits and further legal trouble.

# Ethics 

As a combat focused game, stylised combat is heavily emphasised upon, due to this ethical considerations such as visualising violence well keeping it non realistic is vital. Path of the immortals has avoided gore, and danger towards vulnerable groups and does not allow for any dehumanisation. The choices that have been settled upon are due to the guidelines of the selected age rating being followed, this has included keeping extreme violence and sounds to a minimal while still getting path of the immortals to retain its feel of being a fighter game and stay appropriate to ethical expectations. 

Path of the immortals has aimed to be accessible to all that play it, and have a non discriminatory atmosphere when playing. Despite the playable characters being based on mythological figures, their visual representation is diverse and other aspects of the game are user friendly such as the colour pallets being safe and the game having a readable UI. This has allowed Path of the immortals to be committed to looking after its players wellbeing. 

Most assets within Path of the immortals are original, with the occasional unique referenced source taken from other public domain material, No copyrighted material has been used, and public domain material has been referenced. Mythological characters and backgrounds are open sourced, yet artworks and modern projects are mostly trademarked meaning they have been avoided. 

Overall Path of the immortals has used all legally viable assets, software and original code, the game has ensured external sources have been acknowledged and that no unlicensed assets are present. This has overall ensured the game has stayed professional through development and prepares it for future ethical plans.
