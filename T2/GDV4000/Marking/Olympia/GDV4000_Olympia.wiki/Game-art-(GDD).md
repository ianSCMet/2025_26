# Game sprites

Out of the three characters being looked at, consisting of, Hades, Hermes, and Zeus, due to the game being a one versus one fighting platformer only two characters were decided upon, those being Hermes and Zeus. The main objective was to keep the sprites similar sizes throughout development yet trying to capture Hermes slender figure and Zeus's bulkier figure, linking into their gameplay kits of Hermes being weaker but faster, and Zeus being slower but stronger. Keeping this balance has allowed the game to stay mechanically fair, which was essential in being sure that no playable character had a competitive advantage. Additional changes were implemented during development to improve sprite proportions, and readability, overall developing sprites that communicated each characters role while keeping the game fair.
