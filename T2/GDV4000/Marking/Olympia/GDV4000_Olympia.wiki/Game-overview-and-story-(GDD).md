# Path of the immortals

Path of the immortals is a 2D sprite orientated fighter game which is inspired by Greek mythology. It has been designed to be a game that can be played at either a fast or slow pace, allowing players to decide how they'd like to play the game, the game merges traditional arcade combat with a mythological background, creating a game that is visually distinct and and easily accessible. Each player engages in a one on one versus battle, each battle is different through using unique abilities, animations, and a unique art style that reflects Greek mythology.  

The atmosphere is also intentionally built through audio structure. Consistent with findings that game audio supports informing, entertaining, and immersion, Path of the immortals uses background music for emotional pacing, ambient layers for arena identity, and sound effects for action clarity across key battle events (Andersen et al., 2021, p. 223).

# Path of the immortals background

Path of the immortals follows the Greek mythology narrative heavily, using it's themes and characters as the games background and foundation over using it as a direct adaptation of existing properties. The game uses mythological Gods with unique character designs to the art style being used, with unique move sets and a sprite sheet that is created using ASEprite following the 2D style aesthetic. This approach has allowed the game to use known drama of ancient Greek myths adding to the narrative and ensuring the mechanics and visuals remain unique and legally viable.

# Path of the immortals story 
> Introduction

Path of the immortals takes place during the 2nd millennium BC, where Greek gods were though to have walked the earth, It takes inspiration from Greek mythology and puts its own art style and gameplay spin on it. With Greek Mythology being such a vast subject the lore of Path of the immortals can be seen as very broad, yet it only covers two characters with them both being playable, those being Hermes, and Zeus. Path of the immortals takes place in two arenas, one a fighters coliseum where warriors are known to frequently battle and the other the heavens where Zeus is though to reside. The story is very open, with the players being able to use their imagination and create their own stories through unique and diverse battles. For future implementation more Gods will be added, with the next being Hades, a fan favourite of Greek mythology deepening the story further. Zeus and Hermes go to battle over whatever situation the player decides to think of, an example of this could be a battle to prove who is stronger. With an open ended story it allows for a lot more imagination to be put into the story and for players to easier follow on with the gameplay and story.
