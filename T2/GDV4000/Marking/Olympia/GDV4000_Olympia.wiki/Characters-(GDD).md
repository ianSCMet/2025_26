# General character description

In path of the immortals there are playable characters that being Hermes, and Zeus. Each character has completely unique mechanics yet follow the same gameplay pattern, each character also provides their own twist on the similar gameplay, with Hermes firing a fast paced arrow that does lesser damage and Zeus throwing a slow paced thunderbolt that does high damage, different mechanics have been implemented to keep the playable characters balanced. The difference in the characters abilities allow for the players need of strategy, making aspects such as timing, and risk management vital. By designing different abilities that work more efficiently in different scenarios, it is ensured that the characters have no advantages to retain balance, instead it rewards players for the knowledge they have of the character that they are playing, allowing each playable character to feel unique and fair.

# Original Character ideas

Originally Path of the immortals had three characters, Hades, Hermes, and Zeus, with the game being early access only two characters were decided upon for the original beta, that being Hermes, and Zeus, this being because both have very different possible kits, with Hades being one of the most popular amongst Greek Mythology, it made sense to keep Hades as a future development to retain a sense of excitement for the future of Path of the immortals. Both playable characters were originally going to be the same speed yet have different abilities, yet the trial of making both playable characters very different to each other made it much more competitive which is what was being aimed for.

For each character, unique audio and sound effects will be presented upon the use of light attacks, heavy attacks, and jumping, ensuring each input feels consistent and unique. Originally the intention was to reuse shared audios from generic sources across both characters, yet this failed to capture the essence of what each character was attempting to portray. Implementing character specific sound effects and audio which was more efficient in improving sound quality during a match.

The original art style of Path of the immortals was two arena fighters with stickmen as the playable characters with certain normal attacks that hit harder than others acting as the light and heavy attack system, this was soon changed as the aesthetic of Greek Mythology was found and offered stronger visuals and immersion for character design as a fighter game. The stickman approach was decided upon for the purpose of efficiency, but it quickly became clear that art direction was required, in this case Mythological art direction to provide further clarity. As a result of moving on to a Greek Mythology art style gameplay and readability became significantly more effective. 

# Playable character; Hermes

Hermes is a fast paced character that can traverse the battlefield and attack much quicker than their counterpart playable character, Hermes heavy attack allows him to uppercut with his weapon creating a light effect upon use, this attack will have a cooldown yet upon a successful hit will slow the other player, therefor further playing into his speed. Hermes overall kit allows for fast positioning and offence, rewarding players who can exploit their opponent before they can react. Hermes is designed to keep constant pressure on his opponent, forcing the opponent to play defensively, heavily limiting the other player as to what they can do offensively. Due to this Hermes proves consistent in momentum encounters, when speed and fast decision making can turn the battle, due to this he will be favoured by players who favour precision and agility.

# Playable character; Zeus

Zeus unlike his counterpart Hermes, follows the same combo patterns yet is a slow paced heavy hitter. Zeus can throw thunderbolts that traverse the battlefield slowly yet deal heavy damage if hit, and with Zeus's heavy attack the ground is slammed with a trident creating a lightning explosion dealing AOE damage to its surroundings, including destroyable environments. All of Zeus's mechanics rely on the player being able to smartly play with the slow playstyle and with that high risk comes high reward due to him being able to output a lot more damage than Hermes in a single hit. Overall Zeus's kit allows for strong defence and high reward for opponents making mistakes, potentially seen as the harder of the two characters to play, yet still viable. 

# Cutscenes 

Both fighters will appear walking onto screen from each edge in a cutscene, with no player being able to move their character until a countdown of three seconds has commenced exiting the cutscene. Both Zeus and Hermes will have their animations randomised upon entering the arena within the cutscene with them ranging from their normal, light, ranged, or heavy attack. A cutscene will also commence once the fight is over, and only the victor will have an animation with them walking of screen.
