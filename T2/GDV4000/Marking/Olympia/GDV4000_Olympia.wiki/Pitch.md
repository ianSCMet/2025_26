# Pitch

## Gameplay

### 1. Gameplay

Path of Immortals is an arcade fighter where players player as gods in rounds and are graded at the end of round based on damage, kills, deaths and combos done. Each round will have a time limit from 1 – 3 minutes where players must fight 1v1.  After the final round the total stats will be shown to the players and the winner will be the player with the most kills and damage done.

1.1 Arcade

In this mode after each round each player will have collected points/coins from different stats which can be used to buy combos in a shop presented at the end of each round. [See character section for more information] Each character will have a set of combos ranging from level 1 – 3 which scale in stats and complexity, some combos might require environmental involvement.
While in the shop, players will be presented with 3 cards turned over card, one of each level and players must spend points/coins to purchase these cards. 

### 2. Core Gameplay

Each playable character is equipped with a multitude of combos in which the player can use to defeat the other player. Players can use their environment to their advantage and use objects in the scene to help get the upper hand.

### 3. Gameplay Loop

3.1. Primary loop

Players play in rounds and are graded at the end of round based on damage, kills, deaths and combos done. Each round will have a time limit from 1 – 3 minutes where players must fight 1v1.  After the final round the total stats will be shown to the players, and the winner will be the player with the most kills and damage done.

3.2. Secondary loops

As the rounds go on the map will change an adapt to the players, character who use the environment and have very heavy attacks might damage the environment more causing the players to keep track and be on top of how the use their surroundings.

### 4. Mechanics

- Player will be put in a 2D arena where one large platform will rest at the bottom with some maps including some additional platforms. Player will be able to move left, right, jump, dodge, attack and block. Players will have access to many different combos attached to their character that they will be able to use to fight.

- Different modes would present different challenges and different ways for players to fight. The usage of combos is to avoid strategies such as button mashing as the aim is to get players to think outside the box when fighting.

- Each character in the game with have a set of combos (Level 1, 2 ,3), each character will also be unique in movement based on their class.

### 5. Game Modes

Path of immortals is a 2 player fighting game, releasing with normal and area modes, further modes will be added later such as: Campaign, arcade, endless and challenge modes.

A larger hero roster will also be added, with people such as:

* Hera — marriage, queenship
* Poseidon — sea, earthquakes
* Demeter — harvest, seasons
* Athena — wisdom, strategy, warfare
* Apollo — light, music, prophecy
* Artemis — the hunt, moon
* Ares — war, bloodlust
* Aphrodite — love, beauty
* Hephaestus — fire, forging
* Dionysus — wine, madness, ecstasy
